# 1. Making Recommendations 🎯

Let's start with the most basic question:

> **What exactly is a recommender system?**

A recommender system predicts **what a user might like or want next**.

Examples:

* YouTube → videos you may watch
* Netflix → movies you may like
* Amazon → products you may buy
* Spotify → songs you may enjoy

### A simple example

Imagine we have 3 users and 4 movies:

| User    | Movie A | Movie B | Movie C | Movie D |
| ------- | ------: | ------: | ------: | ------: |
| Alice   |   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |    ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |       ? |       ? |
| Bob     |   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |       ? |   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |       ? |
| Charlie |       ? |    ⭐⭐⭐⭐ |   ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ |       ? |

The question is:

> **What should we recommend to Alice?**

The system looks at the information available about users and items and tries to predict what Alice might like.

For example, if Alice and Bob have similar preferences, and Bob loves Movie C, the system may think:

```text
Alice likes similar things to Bob
             ↓
Bob likes Movie C
             ↓
Recommend Movie C to Alice
```

That's the basic idea behind recommendation.

### 🧠 The key idea

A recommender system is essentially trying to learn:

$$
\boxed{\text{User} + \text{Item} \rightarrow \text{Predicted preference}}
$$

Later, we'll learn **two major approaches**:

**Collaborative filtering**
→ learns from the behavior/preferences of many users.

**Content-based filtering**
→ uses characteristics/features of the items themselves.
