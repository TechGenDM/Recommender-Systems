1. CINEMATCH: VECTORIZED COLLABORATIVE FILTERING ENGINE (FROM SCRATCH)
   - Dataset: MovieLens 100K (GroupLens)
     URL: https://grouplens.org/datasets/movielens/100k/
   - Tech Stack: Python, NumPy, Pandas, TensorFlow (tf.GradientTape, tf.Variable), Matplotlib
   - Core Concepts: Joint Matrix Factorization, Item Mean Normalization, Vectorized Binary
     Masking, Custom AutoDiff Training Loop, Latent Distance / Related Item Discovery.
   - Why It Matters: Demonstrates deep mathematical understanding from first principles
     without relying on black-box recommendation packages.

2. CLICKSTREAM REC: IMPLICIT FEEDBACK & NEXT-ITEM RECOMMENDER
   - Dataset: Retailrocket E-commerce / Instacart Market Basket Analysis
     URL: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/retailrocket/ecommerce-dataset
   - Tech Stack: Python, TensorFlow/Keras, SciPy Sparse, Scikit-learn
   - Core Concepts: Binary Interactions (Clicks, Views, Cart additions), Negative Sampling,
     Binary Cross-Entropy Loss, HitRate@K, Precision@K, Mean Reciprocal Rank (MRR).
   - Why It Matters: Over 95% of industrial recommenders operate on implicit interaction
     streams rather than 1-5 star ratings.

3. STREAMPULSE: TWO-TOWER DEEP RETRIEVAL ENGINE WITH VECTOR SEARCH
   - Dataset: MovieLens 1M / Spotify Million Playlist Dataset
     URL: https://grouplens.org/datasets/movielens/1m/
   - Tech Stack: TensorFlow 2.x (Two-Tower / Dual-Encoder), Google ScaNN / FAISS, FastAPI
   - Core Concepts: User Tower & Item Tower Neural Embeddings, L2 Normalization, Dot-Product
     Similarity, Candidate Retrieval vs. Scoring/Ranking, Sub-10ms Approximate Nearest Neighbors.
   - Why It Matters: The industry standard architecture powering YouTube, Netflix,
     TikTok, and Spotify large-scale candidate generation.

4. EMBEDDINGSCOPE: INTERACTIVE 3D LATENT SPACE & ITEM SIMILARITY EXPLORER
   - Dataset: MovieLens Tag Genome / Learned Item Embeddings
   - Tech Stack: Python, NumPy, Scikit-Learn (PCA), Plotly, Streamlit
   - Core Concepts: Dimensionality Reduction, Covariance Eigendecomposition, Explained
     Variance Ratio, High-D to 3D Projection, Semantic Genre Cluster Visualization.
   - Why It Matters: Solves the black-box interpretability problem by giving visual intuition
     into latent factor spaces.

5. FAIRREC: ALGORITHMIC BIAS AUDIT & SERENDIPITY GUARDRAILS
   - Dataset: Synthetic User Interaction Logs + MovieLens
   - Tech Stack: Python, NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn
   - Core Concepts: Popularity Bias Mitigation, Gini Diversity Coefficient, Intra-List
     Diversity (ILD), Epsilon-Greedy Exploration, Filter Bubble Prevention.
   - Why It Matters: Modern production platforms prioritize fairness, diversity, and
     user welfare alongside raw conversion metrics.
